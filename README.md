A newspaper front page that sums up Thursday 19 November 2026 in a single
image: Trump, Xi and Putin hold the first trilateral summit in Shenzhen and
sign a four-point "Declaration toward Peace"; the World Court finds Myanmar
committed genocide against the Rohingya; Anthropic debuts on the Nasdaq in
the biggest IPO ever while the Dow plunges 1,101 points; Bangladesh burns
under a nationwide lockdown; COP31's draft drops fossil-fuel language;
Voyager 1 is confirmed one light-day from Earth; and Iran's IRGC says two
tankers hit mines in the Strait of Hormuz — plus an "IN BRIEF" strip with
the US Senate's 51–48 Iran vote, Somalia expelling the UAE envoy, and
Mongolia's parliament accepting the president's resignation.

Every headline is a verified 19 November 2026 event (sources in
`../dev/STORY-POOL.md` of the build workspace); one-party claims stay
attributed. The image is drawn deterministically by `gen.py` (Pillow, fixed
seed, no network, no timestamps) and written to `entry.png` (1024×1024).
`main.ts` copies `entry.png` into `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`.
